Dedication

To God,

The One who promised, the One who is faithful, and the One to whom all glory belongs.

This book is dedicated to You, as a testimony of Your faithfulness and a reminder that Your promises are worth holding on to.

And to everyone who continues to trust God while waiting for His promises to be fulfilled in their lives:

May these pages strengthen your faith, renew your hope, and remind you that even when you cannot yet see what God is doing, He remains faithful.

He who promised is faithful.

Introduction

The Promise

There are promises we make to God with a sincere heart, believing that we will never turn back, never fail, and never fall short.

I have made promises to God too.

And there were times when I failed to keep them.

There were moments when my faith was weak, when I became discouraged, and when I did not live up to the commitment I had made before Him. I could have allowed those failures to convince me that everything was over—that perhaps I was no longer worthy to hope, to believe, or to receive what God had placed in my heart.

But I discovered something greater than my own faithfulness:
God remained faithful.

This book was not written because I have always been strong. It was not written because I have perfectly understood God's ways or because I have never doubted during the waiting.

It was written because, through all of it, I have continued to see one truth:

He who promised is faithful.

When I could not see the answer, He was still God.

When I became tired of waiting, He was still faithful.

When I failed, His faithfulness did not fail.

And when I wondered whether the promise could still come to pass, I had to learn that God's faithfulness does not depend on my perfection.

This is a story about waiting.

It is about trusting God when the answer has not yet appeared.

It is about holding on to hope when circumstances seem to say otherwise.

And most of all, it is about discovering that the One who makes the promise is greater than the uncertainty of the waiting.

I do not know everything about how God will fulfill every promise He has placed in my heart. But I have learned that I do not need to know every detail to continue believing Him.

My part is to trust Him, to pray, to remain hopeful, and to keep walking with Him.

His part is to fulfill what He has promised in His perfect way and in His appointed time.

That is why these pages are not a testimony of my perfection.

They are a testimony of God's faithfulness.

If you are reading this while waiting for something you have been praying for—something that seems delayed, impossible, or far beyond your reach—I hope these pages remind you of what I have had to remind myself again and again:

God has not changed.
His faithfulness has not changed.
And His promises are still worth holding on to.

Because in the end, it is not about how perfectly we hold on to the promise.

It is about remembering that the One who gave the promise is faithful.

He who promised is faithful.
