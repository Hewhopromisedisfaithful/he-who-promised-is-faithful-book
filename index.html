Dedication

To God,

The One who promised, the One who is faithful, and the One to whom all glory belongs.

This book is dedicated to You, as a testimony of Your faithfulness and a reminder that Your promises are worth holding on to.

And to everyone who continues to trust God while waiting for His promises to be fulfilled in their lives:

May these pages strengthen your faith, renew your hope, and remind you that even when you cannot yet see what God is doing, He remains faithful.

He who promised is faithful.
